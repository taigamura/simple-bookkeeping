Kaji — five light directions

Each direction merges the same two references at a different ratio: @nemuiasaa's calm off-white canvas, floating white cards, generous whitespace and one-mark app icons — and mokumono's electric blue, uppercase monospace and technical rules. Kaji already ships JetBrains Mono for every number and micro-label, so the mokumono half is mostly free; the real decision is how much of the interface goes quiet.
